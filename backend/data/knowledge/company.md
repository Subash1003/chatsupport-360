# About 360 Degree Info

360 Degree Info (360 DEGREEINFO PRIVATE LIMITED) is a website design, web development
and digital marketing company based in Chennai, India. Our tagline is
"If you can think it, we can develop it."

We design and build responsive, SEO-friendly websites, e-commerce stores, custom
software and mobile apps, and we run digital marketing, branding and hosting for
businesses of every size — from a first company website to large portals for
government departments and established brands.

## At a glance

- Based in Chennai, India; serving clients across 13 countries including India, the
  USA, the UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Canada, Australia, the Netherlands, Belgium,
  France, Nigeria, South Africa and New Zealand.
- 18 years in business, a team of about 30 designers, developers and marketers, and
  more than 7,000 projects delivered.
- One partner for the whole lifecycle: design, build, launch, hosting, and the
  marketing that brings traffic.

## What we do

- Website design and redesign (responsive, mobile-first, SEO-ready).
- Web development on WordPress, Joomla, PHP/Laravel and custom stacks; CMS and
  shopping-cart solutions.
- E-commerce website development (WooCommerce, custom carts) for fashion, food,
  grocery, travel, publishing and more.
- Custom software development — CRM, hotel/hostel booking, inventory and other
  line-of-business systems.
- Mobile application development for Android and iOS.
- SEO and digital marketing — search engine optimisation, social media, bulk
  SMS/email/WhatsApp campaigns.
- Branding and graphic design — logos, corporate identity, brochures, packaging.
- Web hosting on our own infrastructure with a 99.9% uptime guarantee.
- Portal development — education, classifieds, news and travel portals.

## How we engage

Most projects are fixed-scope with a written proposal and a milestone schedule.
Ongoing work — SEO, website maintenance and hosting — is billed monthly or annually
as a retainer. Every enquiry starts with a free consultation to understand the
requirement and give a quote.
