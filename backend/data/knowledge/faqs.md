# Frequently Asked Questions

## How long does a website take to build?

A standard business or brochure website is usually 2 to 4 weeks. A CMS or WordPress
site with custom design and content entry is 4 to 8 weeks. An e-commerce store or a
custom web portal is 8 to 16 weeks depending on the number of features and
integrations. You get a firm schedule with the quote.

## How much does a website or project cost?

It depends on scope. As a rough guide: a business website starts around 25,000 INR,
a WordPress/CMS site around 45,000 INR, an e-commerce store around 75,000 INR, a
custom web application or portal around 3,00,000 INR, and a mobile app around
2,50,000 INR. SEO and digital marketing are monthly retainers from about 15,000 INR
per month. You receive an itemised quote after a free consultation. See the pricing
document for details.

## Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. Every enquiry starts with a free consultation — we discuss the requirement,
suggest an approach, and give a written quote with a timeline. There is no charge and
no obligation.

## Do you build on WordPress, or custom?

Both. For most business and content-driven sites we use WordPress or Joomla so your
team can update content easily. For e-commerce we use WooCommerce or a custom cart.
For applications with complex logic or large scale we build custom on PHP/Laravel or a
modern JavaScript stack. We recommend the option that fits your budget and how you
plan to maintain the site.

## Do you do SEO and digital marketing?

Yes. We run search engine optimisation, social media marketing, Google and social
ads, and bulk SMS / email / WhatsApp campaigns. Digital marketing is a monthly
engagement with a reporting dashboard and a monthly review call. We have been doing
this for over 10 years.

## Do you provide web hosting?

Yes. We host on our own infrastructure — 14 servers globally with a 99.9% uptime
guarantee — and can manage domain registration, business email and SSL. Hosting is
billed annually and can be bundled with a maintenance plan.

## Who owns the website and the code?

You do. On final payment, the website, source code, design files and all accounts
(domain, hosting, CMS) are handed over to you. We keep no rights beyond what is needed
to support the site while you are on a maintenance plan.

## What happens after the site goes live?

You can take a Website Maintenance & Support plan covering content updates, security
patches, plugin and CMS upgrades, backups, uptime monitoring and a guaranteed
response time. Plans are billed monthly and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice.

## Can you redesign or take over an existing website?

Yes. We do redesigns, WordPress/Joomla fixes and migrations, and takeovers of sites
built by someone else. A takeover starts with a short review so we can tell you
honestly what shape the site is in before proposing a plan.

## Which countries do you work with?

We work with clients across 13 countries. Communication is in English. For clients
outside India we schedule calls in your business hours and share written updates
otherwise.

## How do we get started?

Send an enquiry through the website, WhatsApp, or the chatbot's "Talk to a human"
option. Our team replies within one business day to set up the free consultation.
