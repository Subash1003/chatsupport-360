# Policies

## Support and response times

Support is available on a Website Maintenance & Support plan. Target first-response
times by ticket priority:

- Urgent (site down, checkout broken, or a security incident): 2 business hours.
- High (a key page or feature broken, no workaround): 1 business day.
- Medium (minor bug or content issue): 2 business days.
- Low (question or cosmetic change): 3 business days.

Business hours are Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:30 IST, excluding Indian public
holidays. Maintenance plans can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice.

## Hosting uptime

Hosting on our infrastructure carries a 99.9% monthly uptime target. Planned
maintenance is notified in advance and scheduled outside peak hours. Daily backups
are retained for 30 days and can be restored on request.

## Data protection

Client data is used only to deliver the engagement. Access is limited to the assigned
team. Passwords, API keys and gateway credentials are stored in server configuration,
never in the website code, the page delivered to visitors, or source control, and are
never logged. On request we sign a mutual NDA before sharing requirements.

## Intellectual property

On final payment, the website, source code, design files and all accounts (domain,
hosting, CMS logins, ad accounts) are handed over to the client. 360 Degree Info
keeps no rights except what is needed to support the site while a maintenance plan is
active. Licensed third-party themes or plugins remain under their own licences.

## Warranty

Fixed-scope work carries a 30-day warranty from go-live: anything that does not match
the agreed proposal is fixed at no charge. New requests, content changes and
third-party breakages are handled as change requests or under a maintenance plan.

## Refunds and cancellation

A project can be paused or cancelled with written notice. Work completed and in
progress up to that date is invoiced. Advance payments already consumed by completed
milestones are non-refundable. Hosting and retainers are not refunded for the current
billing period but simply not renewed.
