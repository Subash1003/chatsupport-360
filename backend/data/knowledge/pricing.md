# Pricing and Engagement Models

All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and are indicative starting points. The binding
number is the quote issued after a free consultation, which depends on scope, design
effort, number of pages or screens, integrations and content.

## Indicative project pricing

- Website Design & Redesign: from 25,000 INR. Responsive, mobile-first design, up to
  a handful of pages, contact form, basic on-page SEO. Typical delivery 2 to 4 weeks.
- Web Development (CMS / WordPress / Joomla): from 45,000 INR. Custom theme, content
  management, blog, enquiry forms, multi-language where needed. 4 to 8 weeks.
- E-commerce Website Development: from 75,000 INR. Product catalogue, cart, payment
  gateway, order management, on WooCommerce or a custom cart. 8 to 12 weeks.
- Custom Software Development: from 3,00,000 INR. CRM, booking, inventory and other
  line-of-business systems, with roles, dashboards and reports. 10 to 20 weeks.
- Mobile Application Development: from 2,50,000 INR. Android and iOS, published to the
  stores, with a supporting admin panel.
- Portal Development (education, classifieds, news, travel): from 2,50,000 INR.
- Branding & Graphic Design: from 8,000 INR for a logo; corporate identity, brochures
  and packaging quoted per deliverable.

## Ongoing services (billed monthly or annually)

- SEO & Digital Marketing: from 15,000 INR per month. SEO, social media, ads and
  bulk SMS/email/WhatsApp, with a monthly report and review call. Minimum 3 months.
- Website Maintenance & Support: from 3,000 INR per month. Content updates, security
  patches, CMS and plugin upgrades, backups, uptime monitoring, guaranteed response.
- Web Hosting: from 4,000 INR per year. Shared hosting on our infrastructure with
  99.9% uptime, business email and SSL; VPS and dedicated options quoted separately.

## Engagement models

- Fixed scope: a written proposal, milestone schedule and fixed price. Best when the
  requirement is clear.
- Monthly retainer: for SEO / digital marketing, maintenance and hosting, billed in
  advance.
- Dedicated resource: a designer or developer allocated to you full-time on a monthly
  rate for an evolving programme of work. Minimum one month.

## Payment terms

Fixed-scope projects are billed against milestones — typically an advance on
confirmation, a payment at design sign-off, and the balance on go-live and handover.
Retainers and hosting are billed in advance. Invoices are due within 15 days.

## Consultation

The initial consultation and quote are free. There is no charge until you confirm the
project.
