# Technologies and Capabilities

## Website and CMS

WordPress and Joomla for content-managed business sites, with custom themes rather
than off-the-shelf templates. HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript for the front end, mobile-
first and responsive by default. Page speed, on-page SEO and accessibility basics are
part of every build.

## Web development

PHP and Laravel for custom applications and portals, and a modern JavaScript stack
(React, Node.js) where the project needs a rich single-page interface. REST APIs for
integrations. Content, classifieds, news and travel portals with search, listings and
user accounts.

## E-commerce

WooCommerce for most stores, and custom shopping-cart development where the catalogue
or checkout is unusual. Payment gateway integration (Razorpay, PayU, CCAvenue,
Stripe, PayPal), shipping and tax rules, coupons, and order and inventory management.

## Custom software

Line-of-business systems — CRM, hotel and hostel booking, appointment scheduling,
inventory — with role-based access, dashboards and exportable reports. Built on
PHP/Laravel or MySQL-backed custom stacks.

## Mobile applications

Android and iOS apps, cross-platform where it fits the budget, with a web admin panel
and app-store submission handled for you.

## Databases and hosting

MySQL for transactional data. Hosting on our own infrastructure — 14 servers across
regions with a 99.9% uptime guarantee — plus domain registration, business email,
SSL and daily backups. VPS and dedicated servers for larger sites.

## SEO and digital marketing

Keyword research, on-page and technical SEO, link building, Google Business Profile,
Google Ads and social ads, social media management, and bulk SMS, email and WhatsApp
campaigns. Monthly reporting dashboards.

## Design

Logo and identity design, brochures, flyers, newsletters, 3D and packaging design,
and UI design for websites and apps using industry-standard tools.

## Security and good practice

Parameterised database queries, secrets kept in server configuration and never in the
page or in source control, SSL on every site, regular CMS and plugin updates on
maintenance plans, and off-site backups.
